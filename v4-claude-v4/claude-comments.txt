Step 5 is ready as six files: `storyMarker.js` (rewritten), a new `locate.js`, and updates to `main.js`, `layout.js`, `config.js` and `adaptive.css`. As before, I haven't run it, so please test it on your branch, especially on a real phone.

**Markers**
- **Bigger tap targets:** Each marker has a 44 px hit area around an 18 px dot, in place of the old 10 px circle. On phones the Leaflet zoom buttons also grow to 44 px.
- **Keyboard:** Markers are focusable, with a visible ring and an `aria-label` carrying the title. Enter opens the preview or detail, but I don't expect Space to work, because Leaflet only maps Enter.
- **Selected state:** The selected marker is larger and a different color, not color alone. It stays selected while its preview or detail is open, and clears when you dismiss the preview or change category.
- **Hover:** Hover tooltips appear only where a mouse exists. The old hover popup and its stray timer are gone, so touch screens use only the preview card.

**Map behavior**
- **Filtering:** Changing category now refits the map to the visible markers, with padding. On phones, extra bottom padding keeps markers clear of the preview card and the peek bar (`fitPaddingBottomPhone` in `config.js`, set to 130 px), and the zoom is capped at 16 for single results.
- **Find me:** The new button asks for the browser's location permission only when tapped. It shows your position and accuracy on the map, and nothing is sent anywhere or stored.
- **Overlapping markers:** The app now warns about markers closer than 20 m (console, or on screen with `?debug`). I haven't seen your full CSV, so open the page with `?debug` and tell me what it reports.
