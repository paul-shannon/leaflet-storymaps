Step 4 is ready as five changed files: a new `layout.js`, plus updates to `main.js`, `config.js`, `index.html` and `adaptive.css`. I haven't run it, and the bottom sheet is the part most likely to need adjusting on a real phone.

**Comparing the two layouts on a phone**
- **Panel:** `?layout=panel`. The map fills the screen and the gallery is a bottom sheet. One button cycles Show gallery, Make gallery taller and Hide gallery, with no dragging needed.
- **Toggle:** `?layout=toggle`. A Map | Gallery switch sits under the category bar and swaps the full screen between the two.
- **Visitor control:** The credits dialog now has a "Phone layout" choice that applies immediately and is remembered on that device. With no URL parameter and no saved choice, `defaultLayout` in `config.js` decides, and it's set to `panel`.
- **Desktop:** it is unchanged and still shows the gallery and map side by side.

**Shared by both layouts (phones only)**
- **Marker preview:** Tapping a marker opens a card with the thumbnail, title, artist and an Open button. Open leads to the full detail view. Tapping blank map does nothing, as discussed.
- **Expand map:** A 44 px button on the map hides the header, nav and gallery so only the map shows, and the same button shrinks it back. The map never changes size by accident.
- **Filtering:** Changing category dismisses any open preview.

**What I'd test first**
- **Panel on iOS Safari:** Check that the sheet sits correctly as the toolbar collapses, and that the page doesn't scroll behind the sheet.
- **The expand button:** Check that it doesn't overlap the zoom buttons on a small screen.
- **The map after each switch:** I call `invalidateSize()` after each change, and a grey or misplaced map after a switch would mean that call needs adjusting.
- **Keyboard and screen reader:** Try Tab through the handle, toggle and preview. I did not test this.

**Known gaps**
- **Initial zoom:** The map still fits all markers with fixed padding, so on first load a marker can sit under the preview card or the peek bar. I'd add bottom padding for phones.
- **No dragging yet:** Dragging the sheet isn't supported, only the button.
- **Hover popups:** The 1-second hover popups are unchanged (step 5).
